Priority filter

One filter works on all four priority layers. Set a minimum for each factor to hide what does not matter. Then pick what sorts the list: one factor, or all three together.

Sinking rate

laju penurunan

How many millimeters the ground drops each year. Faster sinking means less time before the street floods at high tide.

Population

populasi

How many people live there, and how many still have no piped water. More people means more harm, and more pumping to replace.

Economic activity

aktivitas ekonomi

How much business and industry is there: low, medium, or high. Factories, malls, and hotels pump the most water, so they matter most for leads and water farming.

Map

Exists

A 3D map of Jakarta. Today it shows the sinking spots as tall red stacks, and it can play the satellite passes like a short film.

Bowl page

Exists

A cut-away 3D model of the ground under one spot: soil layers, buildings, the sea at high tide. Slide the years to see how deep it gets by 2050, with and without the pumping ban.

Bowls desk

Exists

A table of spots. Send an inspector on a date, then write down what they found: a pump, no pump, or soil that sinks on its own.

New page and background work

Area check

New page

For people who buy or sell a house: type an address and see if the area sinks slowly, medium, or fast. Areas, not single houses. It never says "do not buy". It also has the water farming calculator.

Groundwater use estimate

Background

We guess how much water an area pumps in two ways: from the people who live there without pipes, and from how fast the ground sinks. If the ground says much more, someone pumps more than we know.

Pass email

Background

One short email every 12 days, after each satellite pass, with only the changes that matter for your job.
